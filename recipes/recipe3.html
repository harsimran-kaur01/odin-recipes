Peanut Butter Cookie

Ingredients

  • 1 cup peanut butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • Parchment paper (optional, for baking)

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Line a baking tray with parchment paper.
  3. Mix peanut butter + sugar in a bowl.
  4. Add the egg and mix until smooth.
  5. Scoop small portions of dough (about 1 tbsp each).
  6. Roll into balls with your hands.
  7. Place on tray, spaced apart.
  8. Flatten each ball with a fork (make a criss-cross pattern).
  9. Bake for 10-12 minutes.
  10. Cool completely before eating
