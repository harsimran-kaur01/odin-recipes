The 3-Ingredient Strawberry Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 box strawberry cake mix
  • 1 cup whipped topping (like Cool Whip)
  • Powdered sugar (for coating)

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F)
  2. Line a baking tray with parchment paper.
  3. Take a bowl and add the strawberry cake mix.
  4. Add whipped topping into the bowl.
  5. Mix well until it forms a soft dough.
  6. Scoop small portions of dough (about 1 tbsp each).
  7. Roll into balls using your hands.
  8. Coat each ball in powdered sugar.
  9. Place on tray and slightly flatten them.
  10. Bake for 10-12 minutes, then cool before eating.
